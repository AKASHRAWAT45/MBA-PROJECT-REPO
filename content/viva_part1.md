# Viva Part 1 – Descriptive Answers (Revised — Secondary Data Study)

## Question 1: What is the central focus of your project and what data did you use?

This project examines AI and automation in banking operations management. Unlike an earlier draft, **no primary survey was conducted**. All empirical data comes from **verified secondary sources**: RBI Annual Report 2023-24, EY India reports (2024, 2025), McKinsey Global Banking Review (2025), Deloitte surveys (2022, 2024, 2025), and annual reports of HDFC, ICICI, Axis, SBI, and Kotak Mahindra Bank. Every statistic is cited in DATA_SOURCES.md.

## Question 2: Describe your research methodology.

I used a **descriptive-exploratory secondary research design** with comparative case analysis. Phase 1 extracted industry statistics from EY, McKinsey, Deloitte, and RBI publications. Phase 2 coded five bank annual reports using a structured case protocol. Phase 3 verified each figure against original sources. Analysis tools included Excel, thematic coding, a structured secondary data register (Appendix C), and chart preparation from verified published values. Non-probability purposive sampling selected five banks by disclosure quality.

## Question 3: What are your most significant findings with real data?

**RBI (2024):** 99.8% digital share of non-cash retail payments; DPI index 418.8 (Sep 2023). **EY (2024):** 78% FinServ firms implemented/plan GenAI; 84% cite customer experience impact. **EY (2025):** up to 46% banking ops productivity gain by 2030. **McKinsey (2025):** 15–20% net cost reduction from AI. **HDFC FY2024:** 75%+ digital acquisitions, 90 lakh WhatsApp interactions/month. **ICICI FY2024:** 71% digital trade transactions. Top challenges: insufficient AI expertise (EY) and legacy integration (Deloitte/McKinsey).

## Question 4: How did you compare banks without fabricating scores?

I report **only bank-disclosed metrics** from published annual reports—no subjective composite index. Examples: HDFC **75%+ digital acquisitions**, **90 lakh WhatsApp/month** (HDFC Bank, 2024); ICICI **71% digital trade** (ICICI Bank, 2024). SBI, Axis, and Kotak disclosures are primarily qualitative, which limits numeric comparison—a stated limitation.

## Question 5: What recommendations follow from verified evidence?

Operations-led governance; process mining before automation; invest in data and APIs alongside pilots; phased roadmap with human-in-the-loop for credit/fraud/AML; AI governance for RBI expectations; workforce upskilling; vendor standards; benchmark against EY/McKinsey/RBI published metrics annually. Banks should treat AI as operating model change supported by **documented industry evidence**, not vendor hype alone.
