# Verified Secondary Data Sources

This appendix lists every major statistic used in charts and Chapter 4 analysis.

## Regulatory Sources

1. **Reserve Bank of India.** (2024). *Annual Report 2023-24*. https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/AnnualReportPublications.aspx?Id=1409
   - Digital share of non-cash retail payments: **99.8%**
   - Payment system volume growth: **44%** (2023-24)
   - RBI Digital Payments Index: **418.8** (September 2023), up from **377.5** (September 2022)

## Industry Research — EY India

2. **EY India.** (2024, February 19). *GenAI set to boost Indian Financial Services; potentially add US$80 billion to GVA by 2030*. https://www.ey.com/en_in/newsroom/2024/02/gen-ai-set-to-boost-indian-financial-services-potentially-add-us-dollor-80-billion-to-gva-by-2030-ey-report
   - **78%** of FinServ respondents implemented GenAI or plan pilot within 12 months
   - **84%** cite customer experience impact; **78%** cite cost reduction; **61%** cite innovation

3. **EY India.** (2025, March 12). *GenAI to drive productivity gains of up to 46% in Indian banking ops by 2030*. https://www.ey.com/en_in/newsroom/2025/03/gen-ai-to-drive-productivity-gains-of-up-to-46-percent-in-indian-banking-ops-by-2030
   - Banking operations productivity gain: **up to 46%** by 2030
   - Financial services productivity: **34–38%** by 2030

4. **EY India Consumer Survey** (n=2,030 customers). Reported via industry media, 2024.
   - **59%** expect AI-powered personalized financial guidance from banks
   - **89%** want unified banking services and faster dispute resolution

## Industry Research — McKinsey

5. **McKinsey & Company.** (2025). *Global Banking Annual Review*. https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/financial-services/our-insights/global-banking-annual-review
   - Net **15–20%** aggregate cost base reduction from AI implementation
   - Gross reductions up to **70%** in select cost categories
   - Global bank technology spend: **~$600 billion/year**

6. **McKinsey & Company.** (2024). *Extracting value from AI in banking: Rewiring the enterprise*. https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/financial-services/our-insights/extracting-value-from-ai-in-banking-rewiring-the-enterprise
   - Credit analyst productivity gains: **20–60%** with multi-agent systems

## Industry Research — Deloitte

7. **Deloitte.** (2022). *Global Intelligent Automation Survey*. https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/topics/talent/intelligent-automation-2022-survey-results.html
   - **74%** of organizations already implementing RPA
   - **50%** implementing OCR; **46%** planning AI in next 3 years

8. **Deloitte.** (2024). *2024 Banking & Capital Markets Data and Analytics Survey* (~150 US banking executives). https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/services/consulting/articles/2024-banking-data-analytics-survey-insights.html
   - **62%** considering GenAI investment; **45%** no-code AI; **52%** migrated >50% data to cloud

9. **Deloitte.** (2025). *EMEA Model Risk Management Survey* (87 banks). https://www.deloitte.com/middle-east/en/services/consulting/perspectives/ai-adoption-in-financial-institutions-balancing-growth-and-governance.html
   - **67%** of banks use AI/ML models (2025), up from 56% (2023)

## Bank Annual Reports (Case Data)

10. **HDFC Bank Limited.** (2024). *Integrated Annual Report FY2023-24*. https://www.hdfcbank.com/personal/about-us/investor-relations/annual-reports
    - **75%+** acquisitions digitally driven with straight-through processing
    - **90 lakh** monthly WhatsApp chat banking interactions
    - **75 lakh** PayZapp customers; **16 lakh** SmartHub Vyapar merchants
    - **30+** acquisition journeys; commercial loan in-principle approval **within 30 minutes**

11. **ICICI Bank Limited.** (2024). *Integrated Report FY2023-24*; Performance Review Q4 FY2024. https://www.icicibank.com/about-us/annual-reports
    - **71%** of trade transactions done digitally in FY2024
    - Trade Online volume **+29.2%** YoY; iLens end-to-end digital lending platform
    - AI chatbots on iMobile Pay; Video KYC for **22 products**

12. **State Bank of India, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank.** Annual Reports 2023-24 — digital platform and modernization disclosures (qualitative case analysis).
